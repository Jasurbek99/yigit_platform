From the greenhouse harvest plan to a completed, paid sale — one truck's journey.

Stage 1 — Planning the harvest

Every block of the greenhouse forecasts how many kilograms it will harvest. We compare plan vs. forecast vs. what actually came in.

PLANNED
🗓️

Weekly plan

Block managers set targets for the week (by Friday).

FORECAST
🔮

Daily forecast

The evening before, the loading dept estimates tomorrow's harvest.

ACTUAL
✅

What came in

Real kilograms once the trucks are filled.

A standard truck holds 18,500 kg  →  forecast ÷ 18,500 = how many trucks to send.

Stage 2 — Building a truck (a "shipment")

A shipment is built by two people who fill in two halves — then the halves are joined into one ready truck.

🌱 LOADING DEPARTMENT  (morning)

The supply half

  • ✅ Which greenhouse blocks
  • ✅ How many kilograms
  • ❔ Buyer & country — not yet
＋
🌍 EXPORT MANAGER  (late morning)

The destination half

  • ✅ Country & city
  • ✅ Customer & buying firm
  • ❔ The tomatoes — comes from supply
🔗 The two halves are joined → one complete truck, ready to ship.

A truck can't leave until it has both the tomatoes and a confirmed buyer & country.
